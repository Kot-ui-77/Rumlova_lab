We are excited to share that our research group has been awarded a new three-year grant from the Czech Science Foundation (GAČR)!

The project, “Mechanisms of DHX15 Capture and Reprogramming by the Mason–Pfizer Monkey Virus G-Patch Module”, will be led by prof. Michaela Rumlová.

Over the next three years, we will explore how M-PMV interacts with and reprograms the cellular RNA helicase DHX15.

We look forward to the new research and discoveries ahead!
