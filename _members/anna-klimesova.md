Anna studied Pharmaceutical Biotechnology at the University of Chemistry and Technology Prague (UCT Prague), receiving her Bachelor's degree in 2020 and Master's degree in 2022.

She currently works as a laboratory technician and contributes substantially to the day-to-day operation of the lab. Her work includes extensive qPCR-based analyses and experimental support across the group's research projects. She is also involved in research focused on flaviviruses.

Anna is a key part of the team, combining hands-on experimental work with keeping the laboratory running smoothly and always being ready to help others with their experiments.
